# Project Features

The Student Management System includes a `Student` structure with the following fields:

- `int studentId` – Unique student identification number.
- `char studentName[100]` – Student full name.
- `int age` – Student age.
- `float cgpa` – Student CGPA.
- `char department[50]` – Student department name.
- `char email[100]` – Student email address.
- `char phone[25]` – Student phone number.
- `char address[200]` – Student address.
- `char gender[10]` – Student gender.
- `int semester` – Current student semester.
- `int creditHours` – Completed or registered credit hours.
- `char status[20]` – Student academic status such as Active, Inactive, or Graduated.

## Improvements

- Student name capacity increased from 50 to 100 characters.
- Address capacity increased from 100 to 200 characters.
- Phone number capacity increased from 20 to 25 characters.
- Student ID field renamed from `id` to `studentId`.
- Student name field renamed from `name` to `studentName`.
